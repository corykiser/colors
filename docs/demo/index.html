Palette Completion Studio

Pick the colors you already have. The model proposes colors to add, running entirely in this page: a set transformer trained by flow matching on rated Adobe Kuler palettes and Amazon Mechanical Turk-rated themes, reranked by a preference scorer trained on a subset of the O'Donovan 2014 dataset (528,106 ratings in total).

Proposals

Your fixed colors come back unchanged and outlined. Tap any proposed swatch to keep it and ask for more.

Swatches are displayed at equal area on a neutral background. Each request draws 32 candidate completions, drops any that leave the sRGB gamut or duplicate a color, ranks the rest with the scorer, and returns a spread of the best. Palettes of up to five colors in total.
